[0:00.00] title
Proceeds Auto-Forward. Sell something, and the money moves from your hot wallet to your cold wallet by itself. No bot. No server.

[0:08.43] problem
Picture an NFT flipper. Sale proceeds pile up in a hot burner wallet. Sweeping by hand gets forgotten, and a keeper bot needs babysitting. Our fix is event-driven, and fully on-chain.

[0:20.19] architecture
Here is the flow. A token transfer or a marketplace sale pays the vault on Sepolia. Reactive Network sees the log, and our ProceedsReactive contract on Lasna requests a callback. The vault applies the owner's rules, and forwards the money to the main wallet.

[0:35.58] code_subscribe
Step one: subscribe. Each route is a Reactive subscription to Transfer or Sale events, whose recipient, topic two, is the vault.

[0:44.56] code_react
Step two: react. It decodes the token and amount, checks that the vault is registered, and requests a callback to the vault.

[0:52.75] code_vault
Step three: on Proceeds. Only the callback proxy, with our reactive contract's ID, can call it. It de-dupes, then checks pause, allowlist, minimum, keep, and gas buffer. A rule miss is an event, never a revert.

[1:07.42] tests
Forge test runs the whole loop locally, with mocks. Thirty-nine out of thirty-nine pass, including a fuzz test: a forward never exceeds the reported amount, or the vault's balance.

[1:30.04] loop2_s3

[1:36.74] loop3_s1
Loop three: a forty-dollar marketplace sale emits two matching logs, a Transfer and a Sale.

[1:43.51] loop3_s2

[1:48.51] loop3_s3
The vault forwards thirty-nine exactly once, and skips the duplicate. No double spend.

[1:54.25] loop4_s1
Loop four: a sale paid in native ETH.

[1:59.25] loop4_s2

[2:04.25] loop4_s3
The vault forwards the ETH, but never touches its gas buffer, which pays the callback fee.

[2:10.37] results
